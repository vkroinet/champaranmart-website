Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Champaran Mart app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the Champaran Mart customer and admin apps and this website.

Information we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address and phone number, provided when you create an account.
  • Delivery addresses — addresses you save (labelled Home, Work, Hotel or Other), including the area, city, pincode and, when provided, a receiver's name and phone number for deliveries to someone else.
  • Location data — with your permission, your device's GPS location, used to detect your current location and suggest a delivery address. Location search and address lookup are powered by Mappls (MapmyIndia).
  • Order information — items, quantities, prices and order status, so we can fulfil and let you track your orders.
  • Usage data — basic app diagnostics needed to keep the service running reliably.

How we use this information

  • To create and manage your account and process your orders.
  • To determine delivery addresses and estimate delivery times.
  • To provide customer support when you contact us.
  • To keep the app secure and working correctly.

How we store and share information

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database and Cloud Storage). We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the Champaran Mart admin team, solely to fulfil and deliver your orders, and with our infrastructure providers (Google Firebase, Mappls) strictly to operate the app.

Your choices

You can edit or delete saved addresses at any time inside the app. Location access can be turned off from your device settings; the app will still work, but you will need to search for your address manually. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at the email below.

Children's privacy

Champaran Mart is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date above.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Write to support@champaranmart.com.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Champaran Mart is a quick-commerce grocery service. This policy explains how we deliver your orders.

Delivery area

We currently deliver within the Champaran & Muzaffarpur region, Bihar. Availability depends on your saved delivery address; if you're outside our service area, the app will let you know at checkout.

Delivery time

We aim to deliver orders quickly, typically within about 30 minutes of placing an order, shown in the app as an estimated delivery time. This is an estimate, not a guarantee, and can vary with distance, traffic, weather and order volume.

Delivery charges

Any delivery fee applicable to your order is shown in the app before checkout, as part of your order summary.

Order tracking

Once your order is placed, you can track its status (placed, packed, out for delivery, delivered) directly in the app under Orders.

Failed or delayed delivery

If a delivery is delayed, refused, or cannot be completed because the address or receiver contact details are incorrect, our delivery partner will attempt to reach you using the phone number provided at checkout. Please keep your contact number and address up to date.

Issues with a delivered order

If an item is missing, incorrect or damaged when delivered, contact us within 24 hours of delivery — see Contact us below — and we will do our best to resolve it, including a replacement or refund where appropriate.
